## power-bi-business-analytics-portfolio
Power BI Business Analytics Portfolio Interactive dashboards and data-driven insights across HR, Global Companies, and Indian Financial Markets.

# 01 - HR Attrition & Workforce Analytics
Analyzes employee attrition, demographics, job roles, departments, compensation, satisfaction, and other workforce factors to identify key drivers of employee turnover.

# 02 - Global Top 2000 Companies Analysis
Analyzes the world's leading companies across countries and industries using financial and business-performance metrics to identify market leaders, opportunities, and strategic insights.

# 03 - Indian Stock Market Analytics
Uses Python-based web scraping to collect Indian stock-market data, prepares and transforms the dataset, and uses Power BI to analyze stock performance, gainers/losers, volatility, market trends, and potential opportunities.
